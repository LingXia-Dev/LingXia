# warm

Warm paper surfaces, ink-brown text and a terracotta accent, for reading-heavy apps.

- Writes the surfaces, lines, text, status colours, scrim, media and shadow in both appearances; the
  shadow is a deep brown in light, so a raised card does not cast grey on cream.
- The accent seed `#b6461c` reads as text at 4.5:1 on the canvas, the sunken surface and the soft
  fills; its family, focus, hues and chart colours are derived from it.
- Danger is a crimson-rose about 30 degrees from the accent, so Save and Delete never read as one colour.
- Type is one step larger than the default (body 17) with looser leading; corners are rounder (12).
  Family, spacing and motion are the default's.

To see the gallery under it: `node ../../scripts/gallery-with-pack.mjs warm`.
