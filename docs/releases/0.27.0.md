# LingXia 0.27.0

## Writing an lxapp

### let guest lxapps get attention for themselves

Guest lxapps can use lx.host.notification and lx.host.banner for themselves (ids, targets and permission prompts scoped to the caller; their banners show the lxapp's name and icon), and come forward with the new lx.surface.bringToFront after the person picks an action on their banner.
